hearing Aural

Executive Summary

An audio-first learning device — designed to train the mind to learn through listening, building deep language connections one sound at a time.

We designed Aural around one simple truth: children learn best when they feel safe. Starting from their own mother tongue, Aural gently opens the door to any new language.

Design Philosophy

sentiment_satisfied
Friendly & Approachable Soft corners, rounded edges, and high-contrast labels reduce intimidation.

Compact & Portable Fits comfortably in a bag for on-the-go learning.
touch_app

fitness_center The Learnability Gym

Executive Summary

The Learnability Gym is a structured system that treats learning as a permanent change in how people act — not just what they know.

It maps real-world competencies into deliberate practice routines that teams repeat until new behaviours become habit. Every routine has a behavioural target. Every session generates measurable evidence.

Design Principles

check_circle
Capacity-Seeding Full capability is transferred to internal champions. The programme outlasts any external engagement.
group
Leadership Goes First Leaders participate in every routine alongside their teams — not as observers.
trending_up
Compounding Design Each phase builds on the previous, expanding capability until behaviours are self-sustaining.

Training Pillars

psychology

Competency Frameworks

Mental models mapped into structured competency clusters — attention, systems thinking, collaboration — tailored to the organisation's context.

energy_savings_leaf

Presence & Energy

Training the often-overlooked capacity to show up with conviction — the difference between being heard and being dismissed.

repeat

Deliberate Practice Circuits

Structured routines for real scenarios — navigating setbacks, triaging overload, resetting expectations — practised until automatic.
